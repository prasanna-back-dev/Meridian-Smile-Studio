# Meridian Smile Studio — Content Strategy

## Content Mission

Educate potential patients about cosmetic dentistry in a way that builds trust, demonstrates expertise, and makes booking a consultation feel like the natural next step — not a sales pitch.

---

## Content Principles

1. **Specificity over generality.** Real numbers, real processes, real credentials. Every piece of content should pass the "competitor swap test" — if you can swap "Meridian" for a competitor name and it still reads fine, the content isn't specific enough.

2. **Patient-first, not procedure-first.** Lead with what the patient wants to achieve, not what the practice offers. "Get the smile you've been thinking about" beats "We offer porcelain veneers."

3. **Honest about what's possible.** Include realistic timelines, costs, and limitations. Transparency builds trust; overselling erodes it.

4. **Educational, not promotional.** Teach patients how to evaluate cosmetic dentistry (any practice), then let Meridian's expertise speak for itself.

---

## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: Procedure Education

**Purpose:** Help patients understand what each treatment involves, who it's for, and what to expect.

| Content Type | Topics | Frequency |
|-------------|--------|-----------|
| Service pages | Veneers, Invisalign, Whitening, Bonding, Smile Makeover | Static (update quarterly) |
| Blog posts | Process explainers, comparisons, FAQs | 2x/month |
| FAQ section | Common questions per service | Static (update monthly) |

**Tone:** Clinical accuracy without jargon. Explain like you're talking to a smart friend who doesn't work in dentistry.

### Pillar 2: Patient Stories

**Purpose:** Social proof that builds emotional connection and demonstrates real results.

| Content Type | Topics | Frequency |
|-------------|--------|-----------|
| Testimonials | Specific patient experiences with details | 3 on homepage, rotate quarterly |
| Before/after gallery | Labeled cases with treatment details | Update monthly |
| Case studies | Detailed patient journeys (with consent) | 1 per quarter |

**Tone:** First-person patient voice. Specific details (treatment type, timeline, cost range). Honest about the process.

### Pillar 3: Expert Authority

**Purpose:** Establish Dr. Cross as a recognized expert in cosmetic dentistry.

| Content Type | Topics | Frequency |
|-------------|--------|-----------|
| Blog posts | Industry insights, technique explanations | 1x/month |
| Credential explainers | AACD, Kois Center, Invisalign Premier | Static |
| Process documentation | Digital smile design workflow | Static |

**Tone:** Authoritative but approachable. Dr. Cross writes like a knowledgeable friend, not a textbook.

### Pillar 4: Local Connection

**Purpose:** Tie Meridian to San Francisco and the Financial District community.

| Content Type | Topics | Frequency |
|-------------|--------|-----------|
| Location content | Financial District, BART access, parking | Static |
| Community posts | Practice news, team updates, office photos | 1x/month |
| Local partnerships | Referral relationships with local businesses | As needed |

**Tone:** Warm, neighborly, specific to SF.

## Content Quality Standards

### Every Piece Must

- [ ] Pass the competitor swap test (specific to Meridian)
- [ ] Include at least one real number or specific detail
- [ ] Have a clear CTA (book consultation, read more, call)
- [ ] Be written in Dr. Cross's voice (or attributed to her)
- [ ] Include internal links to relevant pages
- [ ] Have proper heading hierarchy (H1 → H2 → H3)
- [ ] Be optimized for the target keyword (title, H1, meta, body)

### Never

- [ ] Use lorem ipsum or placeholder text
- [ ] Use generic stock photos
- [ ] Include invented metrics or testimonials
- [ ] Use AI buzzwords (seamless, innovative, cutting-edge, etc.)
- [ ] Publish without Dr. Cross's review and approval
