## How to Report an Issue

### Step 1: Identify the Issue

- What page is affected?
- What device/browser are you using?
- What did you expect to happen?
- What actually happened?
- When did the issue start?

### Step 2: Contact the Right Person

- **Technical issue** (site down, broken form, security) → Developer
- **Content issue** (wrong text, outdated info) → Developer
- **Design issue** (layout broken, colors wrong) → Designer
- **Marketing issue** (SEO, analytics, GBP) → Marketing

### Step 3: Provide Details

Include in your message:
1. Screenshot of the issue (if visible)
2. URL of the affected page
3. Steps to reproduce the issue
4. When you first noticed it
5. How urgent it is (critical / high / medium / low)

- **When:** Sundays, 2:00 AM – 6:00 AM PT
- **Notice:** 48 hours in advance via email
- **Duration:** Typically 1–2 hours
- **Impact:** Site may be briefly unavailable during deployment
