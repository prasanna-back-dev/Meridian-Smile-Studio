What We Do

Three things, done exceptionally well.

No menu of thirty procedures. We focus on veneers, clear aligners, and full smile design because that's where the artistry matters most.

Core Specialty

Porcelain Veneers

Custom-milled, ultra-thin shells bonded to your teeth for a natural, luminous finish. Every set is designed in 3D to match your facial structure, gum line, and desired shade. We use lithium disilicate for strength and translucency that mimics real enamel.

Clear Alignment

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Straighter teeth without brackets or wires. We use iTero scanning for precise digital impressions and ClinCheck software to map every stage of movement before you begin. Most cases complete in 6 to 14 months.

Full Smile Makeovers

Multiple treatments combined into one cohesive plan. Veneers, bonding, whitening, and alignment sequenced together so every piece works as a system.

Professional Whitening

Clinical-grade gel in custom-fitted trays. Lifts deep staining from coffee, wine, and age in a single session. Results last 12 to 18 months with proper care.

Digital Smile Design

See your new smile on screen before committing. We photograph your face, overlay proposed changes, and let you approve the look before any treatment begins.

Flexible financing available. Veneer treatments start at $1,200 per tooth. We partner with CareCredit and Lending Club for 0% interest plans up to 24 months. Full smile makeovers are custom-quoted after your digital preview consultation.

The Dentist

Dr. Elena Cross

Dr. Cross opened Meridian Smile Studio in 2009 after completing her DDS at the University of the Pacific Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry. She pursued advanced training in aesthetic reconstruction at the Kois Center in Seattle and earned accredited membership in the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, a distinction held by fewer than 5% of cosmetic dentists nationwide.

Her approach combines digital treatment planning with hands-on craftsmanship. Every veneer case begins with a digital smile design, a physical wax-up, and a trial smile you can wear for a week before committing. She's placed over 4,000 veneers in her career and maintains a 97% patient retention rate.
