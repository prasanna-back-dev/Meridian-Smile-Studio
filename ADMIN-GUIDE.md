## How to Write a Blog Post

### Step 1: Choose a Topic

Good topics answer questions patients actually ask:
- "How much do veneers cost?"
- "Is Invisalign right for me?"
- "What happens during a smile makeover?"
- "How long do veneers last?"

### Step 2: Write the Content

Keep these guidelines in mind:
- Write in your own voice — patients want to hear from you, not a textbook
- Use specific details (numbers, timelines, real examples)
- Keep paragraphs short (3–4 sentences max)
- Include a call to action at the end ("Book a consultation to learn more")

### Step 3: Send to Developer

Email the following:
1. **Title:** (e.g., "5 Things to Know Before Getting Veneers")
2. **Category:** (e.g., "Veneers," "Invisalign," "Practice News")
3. **Excerpt:** 1–2 sentences summarizing the post
4. **Full content:** The complete post text
5. **Photos:** Any images to include (if applicable)

### Step 4: Review

The developer will format the post and send you a preview link for approval before publishing.

## How to Respond to Google Reviews

### Positive Review Template

```
Thank you, [Patient Name]. It was a pleasure working with you on
[your treatment]. We're so glad you're happy with the results.
— Dr. Cross
```

### Negative Review Template

```
[Patient Name], thank you for your feedback. We take every experience
seriously and would like to make this right. Please contact us at
(415) 555-0142 so we can discuss this directly.
— Dr. Cross
```

### Rules

- Respond within 24 hours
- Never argue publicly
- Never share patient information
- Always offer to resolve offline
- Never offer discounts in exchange for review removal

## How to Use Google Business Profile

### Posting

1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com)
2. Select "Meridian Smile Studio"
3. Click **Add Update**
4. Choose post type:
   - **What's New:** General update, tip, or announcement
   - **Offer:** Special promotion or discount
   - **Event:** Upcoming event or workshop
5. Add text, photo, and button (optional)
6. Click **Publish**

### What to Post

| Week | Post Type | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | Educational | "Did you know porcelain veneers can last 15–20 years? Here's how to make them last." |
| 2 | Before/After | Patient result photo with description |
| 3 | Practice Update | New photo, team highlight, office moment |
| 4 | Patient Quote | Short testimonial with service tag |

### Adding Photos

- Go to **Photos** in GBP dashboard
- Click **Add photos**
- Categories: Interior, Exterior, Team, Products/Services
- Add 3–5 photos per month
- Use high-quality images (at least 720px wide)

## How to Check Your Website Analytics

### Quick Monthly Check

1. Go to [analytics.google.com](https://analytics.google.com)
2. Select "Meridian Smile Studio" property
3. Check these numbers:

| Metric | Where to Find | What to Look For |
|--------|--------------|------------------|
| Total visitors | Home → Users | Growing month-over-month |
| Top pages | Engagement → Pages | Which pages get the most views |
| How people find you | Acquisition → Traffic acquisition | Organic search, direct, social |
| Form submissions | Events → Consultation submit | How many consultation requests |
| Phone calls | Events → Phone call click | How many phone inquiries |

### What the Numbers Mean

- **More organic visitors** = SEO is working
- **More direct visitors** = Brand awareness is growing
- **More form submissions** = Website is converting
- **High bounce rate on a page** = Content or design needs improvement
- **Low time on page** = Content isn't engaging enough

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How often should I post on the blog?**
A: Aim for 2 posts per month. One educational post and one practice update or patient story.

**Q: How quickly will I see results from SEO?**
A: SEO takes 3–6 months to show significant results. Consistent content and citation building accelerate this.

**Q: What if I get a bad review?**
A: Respond within 24 hours, acknowledge the feedback, and offer to resolve it offline. Never argue publicly.

**Q: Can I update the website myself?**
A: The website is code-based and requires a developer for changes. Email your requests and they'll be handled within 1–2 business days.

**Q: How do I know if the website is working?**
A: Check GA4 monthly for visitor trends and form submissions. If consultation requests are increasing, the website is doing its job.
