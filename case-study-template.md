### The Challenge

Meridian Smile Studio, a premium cosmetic dental practice in San Francisco's Financial District, needed a website that reflected the quality and specificity of their work. The existing online presence didn't communicate:

- Dr. Elena Cross's AACD accreditation and advanced training
- The digital smile design process that differentiates Meridian from competitors
- The warm, editorial aesthetic that matches the practice's personality
- Transparent pricing and financing options

The challenge was to create a website that feels like a trusted advisor, not a dental template.

---

### The Approach

**Anti-AI-Slop Design Philosophy**

Every design decision was made to avoid the visual patterns that signal "AI-generated" or "template":

1. **Typography:** Fraunces (variable optical serif) instead of overused Playfair Display or Inter
2. **Color:** Warm ivory base (#F7F3EC) with muted brass accent (#A98552) — no navy, no purple gradients
3. **Layout:** Asymmetric grids, pull-quotes, full-bleed photo breaks — no uniform 3-column cards
4. **Copy:** Passes the competitor swap test — specific to Meridian, not generic dental marketing
5. **Specificity:** Real credentials (AACD, Kois Center), transparent pricing ($1,200/tooth), specific timelines

**Content Depth**

Rather than surface-level "About Us" and "Services" pages, the site includes:

- Step-by-step process walkthrough with realistic timelines
- Smile goals quiz (interactive element)
- Detailed competitor analysis informing positioning
- Three buyer personas driving content strategy
- Blog with educational long-form content
- Transparent financing section with specific pricing

**Technical Rigor**

- Semantic HTML5 with ARIA landmarks
- WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility compliance
- Schema.org structured data (Dentist)
- Google Analytics 4 with custom conversion events
- Google Search Console with sitemap
- Vercel deployment with security headers
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Performance target: Lighthouse > 90

---

### The Solution

**Single-page scroll** with all core sections (hero, services, about, process, gallery, quiz, testimonials, CTA, contact) plus a separate blog page.

**Key features:**

1. **Digital smile preview CTA** — The hero headline speaks to Meridian's unique differentiator
2. **Process timeline** — Four steps with realistic timeframes (60 min consultation, 5-day preview, 1-week trial, 2–3 hour placement)
3. **Smile goals quiz** — Interactive 3-step assessment that routes to consultation booking
4. **Transparent pricing** — $1,200/tooth with CareCredit and Lending Club financing
5. **Location as asset** — BART access, parking details, Financial District positioning
6. **Portfolio label** — "Concept design for portfolio purposes" for honest presentation

### Key Takeaways

1. **Anti-AI-slop requires intentional decisions** — Every choice (font, color, layout rhythm, copy tone) was made to avoid default patterns
2. **Content depth builds trust** — Specific credentials, transparent pricing, and realistic timelines differentiate from template sites
3. **Interactive elements add product thinking** — The smile quiz is the kind of feature a template won't include
4. **Local SEO is a long game** — The technical foundation is built; results compound over 6–12 months
5. **Portfolio honesty matters** — Labeling the project as concept design maintains credibility
