## Review Strategy

### Target

- **50+ Google reviews** within 6 months
- **4.8+ average rating** maintained at all times

### Review Generation Process

1. **Post-appointment email** (sent 48 hours after visit):
   ```
   Subject: Thank you from Meridian Smile Studio

   Hi [Patient Name],

   Thank you for visiting us today. We hope you had a comfortable experience.

   If you have a moment, we'd appreciate your honest review on Google. It helps other patients in San Francisco find the right cosmetic dentist for their needs.

   [Leave a Review] button → direct Google review link

   Thank you for trusting us with your smile.

   — Dr. Elena Cross and the Meridian Team
   ```

2. **In-office signage**: Small card at the front desk with QR code linking to Google review page

3. **Follow-up text** (for patients who prefer text):
   ```
   Hi [Name], thank you for visiting Meridian today. If you have a moment, we'd love your review: [short link]. Thank you! — Dr. Cross
   ```

### Review Response Templates

**Positive review response:**
```
Thank you, [Name]. It was a pleasure working with you on [specific treatment]. We're so glad you're happy with the results. — Dr. Cross
```

**Neutral/negative review response:**
```
[Name], thank you for your feedback. We take every experience seriously and would like to make this right. Please contact us at [phone] so we can discuss this directly. — Dr. Cross
```
